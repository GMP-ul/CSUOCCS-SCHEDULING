How to book for an appointment?

1

Get a referral

The applicant must first obtain a formal referral from an authorized office such as DOST, HR Office or your assigned counselor.

2
0101
1010
0101

Obtain the unique code

Upon receiving the referral, you will be provided with a unique code by the referring office. This code is your key to access the appointment system.

3

Access the online testing scheduler

Go to the official OCSS Online Testing Scheduler website and prepare your unique code.

4

Verify the unique code

Enter your unique code to verify your referral. If the code is invalid, please check your code or contact the office that issued the referral.

5

Provide Applicant Information

Enter your unique code to verify your referral. If the code is invalid, please check your code or contact the office that issued the referral.

6

Select an Available Schedule

Choose your preferred appointment date, time, and venue from the available schedules. Availability may be limited and venues may vary depending on the date

7

Review the requirements

Review the requirements before submitting your appointment. Please bring it on the day of your appointment. No need to upload the picture of the receipt.

8

Receive Appointment Confirmation

After submission, you will receive an email confirmation containing your appointment details, venue, requirements and important instructions.

9

Attend the scheduled appointment

Arrive at the designated venue on your scheduled date and time. Bring all the required documents and materials, including receipts if required.

Follow each step carefully to complete your appointment booking.
